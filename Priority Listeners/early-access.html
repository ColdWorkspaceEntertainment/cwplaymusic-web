Get a preview of new CW PLAY tracks before release. It's free: all we ask in return is a short feedback after you listen.

How it works

  1. Find it in Fizzy's catalog. Fizzy shares the store catalog on Discord.
  2. Press Order on Priority Listeners – Early Access.
  3. Wait for approval. Every order is reviewed by hand.
  4. Get your preview in DM before the track comes out.
  5. Send a short feedback about what you heard.

Good to know

  • You get a preview, not the full track.
  • Your preview is personal. Don't share or re-upload it.
  • Spots are limited for each preview round.
  • Breaking the rules removes you from the list.
