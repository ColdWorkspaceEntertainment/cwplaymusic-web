What is CW PLAY?

CW PLAY (Cold Works PLAY) is the music production arm of Cold Works Production. As part of the brand's wider creative universe, we shape its sound and run its virtual cinema.

  • Original productions
  • Regular releases
  • Early previews for Priority Listeners

  • Content that shares our process with viewers
